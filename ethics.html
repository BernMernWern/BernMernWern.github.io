Ethics in Information Technology

IT professionals are responsible for building systems that are fair, safe, and respectful of user rights. The following are two key resources that outline ethical principles for developers, data scientists, and engineers:

  • 🔗 ACM Code of Ethics and Professional Conduct
    Provided by the Association for Computing Machinery, this code emphasizes honesty, privacy, and professional responsibility.

  • 🔗 IEEE Code of Ethics
    Issued by the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers, this code encourages integrity, safety, and accountability in technology development.
